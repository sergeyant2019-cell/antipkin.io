What changes

Four moves, in this order.

  • 01
    Numbers first.Funnel, team, pipeline: where you lose revenue, and why.
  • 02
    Two or three decisions.Segment, offer and pricing, channels, people. Only the ones that change revenue.
  • 03
    A system, not heroics.Process, CRM, hiring, onboarding, AI-assisted lead handling.
  • 04
    Run it or hand it over.I stay as your CRO one or two days a week, or your team runs it without me.

About

I build sales teams at tech companies.

I spent 14 years doing that from the inside. Now I work independently, with a few companies at a time.

At Cian, a real estate marketplace that went public on the NYSE, I went from leading regional sales teams to Sales Director. The lines I ran grew revenue more than 10x and brought in about 60% of the company's total. At Anabar, a SaaS analytics company, I ran business development and stayed on as an advisor and investor.

Yandex Practicum invited me to build its program for heads of sales. I took it from concept to launch, and I still mentor the founders, executives and sales leaders who go through it. I'm also an ICI-certified coach.

When I believe in a company, I take part of my fee in equity and invest my time and experience in it. It's never a condition of working together. I don't run a fund and I don't raise capital.

I live in Belgrade with my family. I work with companies here in person, and remotely elsewhere.

Questions

Before you book.

What happens on the first call?

You tell me how you sell today and where it hurts. Rough numbers are enough. I give you my first read: where I'd look and what I'd check first. If I don't think I can help, I'll say so. Whatever you share stays between us, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before we talk.

Which companies do you work with?

B2B tech companies, SaaS, marketplaces and PropTech where the product already sells, usually through the founder. That's the point where a sales system pays off fastest. I also work with banks and fintech companies building partner sales channels.

How is this different from hiring a Head of Sales?

A senior hire takes months to find and months to prove. I start with a review, build the process and the team, and then either stay on as your part-time CRO or hand over to the head of sales you hire or promote.

Can we hire you full-time?

I mostly work independently. But if the product excites me and the company has real prospects, I'm open to that conversation.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Russian and Ukrainian. I'm learning Serbian and will work in it soon.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. In Belgrade I come to your office. With teams elsewhere I work remotely and travel when it matters to be there in person: hiring, kick-offs, key deals.
